Vanilla look (default).

This subpack is intentionally empty. Selecting it means no tool/weapon attachables
are loaded, so netherite tools and weapons render with the default flat item sprite.

The 3D tool models live in the "actions_and_stuff" subpack and are only loaded when
the player selects the Actions & Stuff compatibility option in the pack settings.

Armor and spear attachables are part of the base pack and are always loaded.
